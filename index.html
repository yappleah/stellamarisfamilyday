About the Celebration

Presented by the Stella Maris Church Fundraising Committee, “Come Home for Christmas” brings together family, friends, and neighbors to celebrate the true joy of the Christmas Season!

Immerse yourself in wholesome fun filled with authentic Jamaican traditions, mouthwatering food catered by Patsy Lynn, live cultural entertainment, kids' attractions, and our evening Tree Lighting Ceremony—all while supporting vital church programs and community outreach initiatives.
